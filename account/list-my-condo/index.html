Investor sale process
Sell to a buyer who keeps the tenant (or negotiates buyout separately). No tour disruption to the tenant.
Tenant buyout logistics (cash-for-keys)
  • Typical SF range: $20K–$30K — varies by tenancy length & rent-control status
  • Built into purchase contract as a credit at close OR funded separately at signing
  • SF Tenant Buyout Ordinance compliance — written disclosures required, 30-day rescission window for tenant
  • Lawyer on retainer drafts the buyout agreement; recorded with SF Rent Board
  • Owner and buyer split cost via negotiation — typically owner-funded but creative structuring possible
