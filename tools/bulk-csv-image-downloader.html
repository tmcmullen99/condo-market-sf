CSV bulk image downloader

Point this at your Webflow CMS CSV export. It parses the image URLs, downloads them all from Webflow's CDN, and packages a ZIP organized by building slug.

Why this works: Your browser can fetch Webflow CDN images directly (they allow CORS for image assets). No proxy needed. No scraping. Just URL list → download → zip.

Steps

  1. Click the file picker, select your SF_Condo_Market_-_Buildings_*.csv
  2. Click Parse CSV — shows building count and total images
  3. Click Download all images — fetches every featured + gallery + floor plan image
  4. Click Build ZIP — packages everything, triggers download
  5. Upload the ZIP to Claude
