How to sell your San Francisco condo off-market

To sell your San Francisco condo off-market, name the price that would make you move, privately. There is no listing, no open house and no lockbox, and your name is never shown. Members of The San Francisco List, including buyers watching your building, can send a written offer through Tim McMullen, and you decide whether to answer. You can change or remove your number at any time, at no cost.

Selling off-market, in five steps

  1. Name your number, privately

    Tell us the price that would make you move. It is never shown with your name, and the unit is shown only to members.

  2. Keep living there

    No listing photos, no open house and no showings unless you agree to one. Nothing changes in the building.

  3. Reach the buyers watching your building

    Buyers watch the buildings they want. When you name a price, the members watching your building hear about it, and can send a written offer through Tim.

  4. Check it against the record

    Every recorded sale in your building is on its page, with the units directly above and below yours. If you want a professional opinion, ask Tim for a Comp Report: free, with no listing required.

  5. Sell, or don’t

    If an offer works, it closes like any sale: contract, HOA documents, inspections, escrow and title. If none does, change or remove your number. No fee and no commitment until you sign.

Selling off-market in San Francisco: questions

Can you sell a condo off-market in California?
Yes. An owner can sell to any buyer without a public listing. The contract, the HOA documents a seller must provide (Civil Code §4525), escrow and title work the same way as in any sale. A licensed agent follows the rules on how a home may be marketed privately, and Tim explains which apply before anything about your unit is shown.
How do I sell my San Francisco condo without listing it?
Name your price on Condo Market SF. The unit is shown only to members of The San Francisco List, never with your name, and any offer reaches you through Tim.
Will I get less selling off-market?
It depends on the unit and on who sees it. A private sale reaches fewer buyers than a public listing, which can mean fewer competing offers; it also avoids showings, listing photos and a public price history. Tim can show you the recorded sales in your building and, if you ask, a Comp Report.
Is my information private?
Your name is never shown on this site, and no address, price or photo from The San Francisco List is shown in public. You can remove your number at any time.
What if my condo has a tenant?
You can sell it with the tenant in place. List it on The Exchange, where investors who want the income look, or read the guide to selling a tenant-occupied condo.
What does it cost?
Naming your price is free and commits you to nothing. If you decide to sell, the terms are agreed in writing with Tim first.
