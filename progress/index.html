Cold Drip · proprietary email warming engine

We built our own warming engine.
It compounds every single day.

Most companies rent reach from Mailchimp, Constant Contact, or — worse — buy attention on Meta and Google. Condo Market built Cold Drip: a purpose-built email warming and pacing system that increases our daily send capacity week over week as our sender reputation strengthens with Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo. Each campaign starts small and grows by a fixed factor; each wave that lands clean unlocks the next. The longer it runs, the more we can reach — and the more durable the channel becomes.

Real schedule from the email_send_caps table. In 8 weeks Cold Drip scales daily capacity 11× — from 300 emails to 3,500 emails per day combined. At steady-state that's up to 105,000 owner conversations per month, all to people we already know by name.

01

The mechanism

Each campaign starts with a tiny warm batch — five to ten emails on day one. Every 24 hours the batch grows by a fixed factor (1.5× or 1.6×). Bounces, complaints, and unsubscribes are tracked per recipient and per building; reputation builds in real-time. The system can't outrun deliverability.

02

The compounding

A warmed sender domain is a long-tail asset. The reputation built sending Wave 1 in May powers Wave 50 in September and Wave 500 next year. Every new campaign starts from a higher floor of inbox trust. The first month is the hardest. The fourth month is free.

03

The data underneath

No purchased lists. Every recipient is an owner in our database — verified email, building, unit, neighborhood, owner type. 1,457 fully validated; 10,689 unit-level contacts; segmented audience views for out-of-state owners, corporate owners, HOA managers.

Building partnerships · the unreplicable advantage

We're not just in the database.
We're in the lobby.

The audience advantage is owned. The Cold Drip ramp scales it. But the truly unreplicable layer of the moat is physical: Condo Market partners directly with each building's HOA to integrate marketing into the lived experience of the community. We get the lobby, the elevator, the mailroom, the resident newsletter, the community events. In exchange, the building's HOA gets 1% of every sale we close in their tower — paid directly to the HOA reserve, no strings, to spend on whatever makes their community better.

The cycle of value

01

Physical presence

Lobby signage. Elevator screens. Resident newsletter sponsorship. Community event support. Condo Market becomes part of the building's daily texture.

02

Owner trust

Residents see us where they live. We're the brand they associate with their own building — not an out-of-town aggregator they encountered in a Google ad.

03

Sales flow through us

When an owner sells, we're the natural choice. We know the building. We know the comps. We know the neighbors. Transactions close faster, at better prices.

04

1% goes to the HOA

One percent of every sale we close flows directly back to the building's HOA reserve. No application process. No special account. No clawback. Just paid.

05

Community gets better

HOA boards invest in what residents actually asked for — gym equipment, rooftop renovations, EV chargers, lobby refreshes, building-wide events. The building becomes more desirable.

06

Property values rise

Better building means higher unit values. Higher unit values means richer sales. Richer sales means more 1% back to the HOA. The cycle compounds.

↻ Every sale strengthens the building. Every improvement raises the next sale. Forever.
