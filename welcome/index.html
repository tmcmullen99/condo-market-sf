How it works

From claim to close, in four steps.

  1. Claim your unit

    Pick your building, type your unit, type the name on title. Tim reviews each claim against title — usually within a few hours during business hours.

  2. Set your make-me-move price (or skip it)

    Optional. If you do set one, it's visible only to verified buyers — never on Zillow, MLS, or anywhere public. Change it anytime, hide it anytime.

  3. The platform sits quietly between you and demand

    Most of the time, nothing happens. That's the point — no cost, no obligation, no listing.

  4. If a buyer hits your price, decide

    Tim emails you when a verified buyer signs a Letter of Intent at your price. You decide on your own time. If yes, Tim handles the Residential Purchase Agreement and escrow as your licensed agent — same close as any traditional sale.
