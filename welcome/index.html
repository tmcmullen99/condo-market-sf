Three things, quietly.

Float your number

How it works

From claim to close, in four steps.

  1. Claim your unit

    Pick your building, type your unit, type the name on title. Tim manually reviews each claim against the title record on file — typically within a few hours during business hours.

  2. Set your make-me-move number (or skip it)

    You don't have to set a number to claim. If you do, it's visible only to verified buyers — never on Zillow, MLS, or anywhere public. Change it anytime, hide it anytime, never use it at all.

  3. Wait. The platform sits quietly between you and demand.

    Most of the time, nothing happens. Which is exactly the point — you carry no cost, no obligation, no listing.

  4. If a buyer hits your number, decide

    Tim sends you a one-line email when a verified buyer signs a Letter of Intent at your price. You decide on your own time. If you say yes, Tim handles the Residential Purchase Agreement and escrow as your licensed agent. Same close as any traditional sale.
