FAQ

Is signing this binding?

No. Signing is a non-binding statement of homeowner support. The petition is delivered to your HOA Board as a representation of homeowner sentiment — they decide whether to engage Condo Market in a formal partnership.

Who sees who signed?

Only verified homeowners of your building can see the list of signers. Aggregate counts ("12 owners have signed") are visible to anyone visiting this page — that's by design, to encourage participation. Your name is never sold or shared with third parties.

How is my ownership verified?

We match your account's full name against public property records (the recorded owner-name on file with the City and County of San Francisco) for units in this building. If your name matches, you're verified instantly. If you bought recently and records haven't updated, contact tim@mcmullen.properties for manual verification.

Can I revoke my signature?

Yes. Revoke at any time before the petition is formally delivered to your HOA Board, using the "Revoke" button after you've signed.

What's in it for the broker?

Condo Market builds a direct relationship with HOAs in exchange for becoming the building's preferred sales platform. The HOA gets 1% of every sale; the broker (McMullen Properties · DRE #02016832) earns the standard listing commission. Both sides win when the partnership works — and you win when the building you live in is better funded.
