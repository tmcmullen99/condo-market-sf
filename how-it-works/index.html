How it works

Interest, not listings.

Every condo in San Francisco is for sale — for the right price. Condo Market is the marketplace where buyers register interest in any unit and owners name the number that would actually move them. When interest meets a number, a California-licensed agent steps in to make it real.

Condo Market SF is a marketing and lead-acquisition platform operated by McMullen Properties · CA DRE #02016832. The Platform itself is not a real estate brokerage and does not perform real estate brokerage activity. Licensed real estate services on Platform-originated transactions are performed by participating California-licensed agents.

01.

Browse the 64 buildings

Every major condo building in San Francisco, mapped and indexed. Filter by neighborhood, price, size, and amenity. Open any building's dossier — sale history, unit counts, price trajectories, ownership patterns.

5,082 condos in the index. Listed or unlisted, every unit is part of the marketplace — that's the point.

02.

Find a unit. Set a number.

You're not limited to whatever's listed today. Any of the 5,082 condos in the index is a candidate. Pick the address you want. Sketch the price you'd actually pay. Note the timing, the financing posture, and the contingencies that matter to you.

None of this is binding. It's the marketplace's job to find out who's interested in what — before anyone drafts a contract.

03.

Hit Express Interest. An agent picks up.

When you're ready to put a real offer on the table, click Express Interest. That click hands the marketplace off to a California-licensed real estate agent — McMullen Properties · CA DRE #02016832, the Platform's designated agent for buyer introductions.

Within 24 hours, your agent calls. They translate the price, terms, and timing you've set into a formal Letter of Intent, walk it through with you line by line, and deliver it to the owner. The LOI is the actual instrument — drafted by your agent, not the Platform.

From the moment you express interest, you have representation.

04.

From LOI to keys

Your agent runs the deal end-to-end: counter-offer rounds, the SFAR Residential Purchase Agreement, escrow opening, disclosures, final walkthrough, close. Standard 30–45 day timeline. The same licensed agent who picked up at step three signs off at closing. You get the keys.

Our designated agents charge a flat 3% commission on Platform-originated transactions. McMullen Properties donates 1% of their commission to the building's HOA on every closed transaction.

01.

Claim your unit

Sign in. Find your building in the index. Claim your specific unit on the building's unit map. We verify the claim against the public ownership record before anything else happens.

Claiming is free, takes about 90 seconds, and obligates you to nothing.

02.

Name your make-me-move number

Set a Make-Me-Move number. Not a listing. Not a promise. Not public. Just the figure that would actually make you reconsider selling. Change it anytime. Pause it anytime. Walk away anytime.

Your unit, your number, your terms. No agent calls you. No marketing nudges. The marketplace waits.

03.

Watch interest arrive on your terms

When buyer interest meets or beats your number, you get an email — and only then. No tire-kickers. No lowballs. No mailing-list noise. The marketplace surfaces only what matters: real, qualified, price-matched interest.

You're not negotiating in a dashboard. You're watching the market come to you.

04.

An agent handles every offer

When the marketplace surfaces real buyer intent on your unit, McMullen Properties · CA DRE #02016832 — the Platform's designated agent for seller introductions — calls you. They present the offer, walk you through accept / counter / pass, draft any counter you decide on, and handle the SFAR Residential Purchase Agreement if you say yes.

From the moment you take an action that requires representation, you have it.

Flat 3% commission on Platform-originated transactions, charged by your designated agent. McMullen Properties donates 1% of their commission back to your building's HOA on close. No upfront cost. No exclusivity agreement. No obligation until you sign the SFAR RPA.
