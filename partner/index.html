How the money flows.

Three steps from your buildings, through our platform, to your firm.

1

You surface us

Your firm promotes Condo Market across the buildings you manage — lobby presence, resident-facing email, newsletter and print where you have it. Residents see the platform as the modern way to list or research a sale.

2

Sales close through the platform

Owners list with us off-market; we match them to buyers and route the transaction through California-licensed agents at a flat 3% fee. No MLS, no chaos, lower friction for residents and your front desk.

3

You earn on close

1% of every closed sale price in your managed buildings is paid to your firm at close. A $1.5M unit closing through us means $15,000 to your firm. You decide where it lands — operating accounts, board reserves, your firm's P&L. We send a single monthly statement.

The terms.

Short, written, and renewable.

Compensation
1% of the final sale price, paid to your firm at close, on every condominium sale in your managed buildings that closes through Condo Market SF.
Discretion
Where the 1% lands is your call — building operating accounts, board reserves, or your firm's P&L. We disburse to whichever account you nominate.
Exclusivity
Non-exclusive on both sides. Owners can list anywhere; you can partner with anyone. No long-term lock-in.
What we ask of you
Active promotion of Condo Market across your portfolio: lobby presence, at least quarterly resident-facing email, newsletter and print where applicable.

Your commitments.

Three lightweight asks. Nothing requires staff time beyond what you already do.

Physical presence
Condo Market signage in lobbies, building newsletters or print collateral once a quarter. We send the materials; your team places them.
Online surface
Mention Condo Market in resident-facing email, board minutes, or community portals. Include link to your firm's dedicated portal page (this one) on annual disclosures.
Introductions
When an owner asks about selling, mention Condo Market as one of the off-market options. We do the rest — no agent recommendations required.

Questions.

Is your firm a brokerage?
No. Condo Market SF is a marketing and lead-acquisition platform. All licensed real estate services are performed by California-licensed agents — currently Tim McMullen (DRE #02016832), operating under Real Broker (Brokerage DRE #02228473).
When do we get paid?
On close, alongside the sale's settlement. Our title coordinator wires the 1% to the account your firm designates within 5 business days of recording.
Do residents have to use Condo Market to sell?
No. Owners always have the option to list with anyone. The partnership pays out only on sales that close through our platform. Your firm benefits when residents choose us; you're not penalized when they don't.
Can boards opt out of the 1%?
Yes. The HOA board for any individual building can opt out of the partnership entirely and the platform will not solicit listings in that building. The 1% incentive is per-firm, not per-board.
What if we already have a relationship with another platform or brokerage?
Non-exclusive. You can promote whoever you want. The 1% only pays on closings through Condo Market — there's no conflict with existing arrangements.
