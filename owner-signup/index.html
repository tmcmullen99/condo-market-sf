Why this works

Not a listing. Not a process.

01

You don't move out.

No staging, no neutralizing, no early relocation. You stay until a number you actually like lands on the table — or forever, if it doesn't.

02

You don't compete.

Your price shows up on your building's page — but only to verified members signed into Condo Market. Never on Zillow. Never in MLS. No public sign, no open house. Your name stays private until you decide to engage with a buyer.

03

Discounted commission.

If a buyer meets your price and you decide to sell, the streamlined service model means a lower commission. Refer five friends and that drops by another 1%.

04

You get the offers.

Setting your number puts your unit in the Featured section of your building's page — where active buyers focus first. Owners without a number get far fewer offers, and the ones they do get tend to be casual at best.
