Building by building

Every catalogued building with its own recorded sales: the last three years’ median $/sf, how it moved, and how often it trades. Pick up to four from the table or the map to chart them by year.

Every figure is a count or a median of recorded condo sales in San Francisco County records and the MLS, for the buildings this market catalogues. Each sale is counted once. A median needs five sales: the price history plots each quarter as the twelve months to it, and neighborhoods and buildings as the three years to each year, so a building that trades a few times a year still has a median; where a year falls short, a dashed line joins the medians either side, and a series without a median in most years is not charted. Sales under $100,000, below-market-rate resales and implausible $/sf are left out. Nothing here is an opinion of what any home is worth. How each figure is calculated · Every building
