How it works

Two sides of the same exchange.

The platform assigns a licensed agent who takes your offer to the owner. You hear back within 24 hours.

01

Browse the index

Every unit across the market’s buildings — whether it's listed on the MLS or not. Tax-record data, floor plans, unit history.

02

Set the number

Pick a unit. Sketch the price, financing posture, and timeline you'd actually move on. Nothing here is binding — yet.

03

Express interest

One click hands the marketplace off to your assigned California-licensed agent. They draft the formal Letter of Intent and deliver it to the owner.

04

Close on your terms

If accepted, your agent runs the SFAR Residential Purchase Agreement and escrow. Flat 3% transaction fee. 1% to participating HOAs.

01

Claim your unit

Verify ownership. Takes two minutes. Your unit appears on the platform — privately, to you.

02

Name your number

Set a Make-Me-Move number. Not a listing. Not an agreement to sell. The figure that would actually make you reconsider.

03

Watch the book

See which buyers are interested and at what price. The marketplace surfaces only what matches or beats your number.

04

Move only if you want to

Your assigned California-licensed agent presents every offer. Accept, counter, decline, or withdraw your number anytime. You commit to nothing until you sign the SFAR RPA.
